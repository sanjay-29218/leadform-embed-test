About Kererū Lane

A small Wellington team of immigration advisers who have made the move ourselves.

Practical advice, plain language

We started Kererū Lane after watching friends struggle through confusing visa rules. Today we help skilled workers, students and families from around the world settle in New Zealand.

We keep our fees fixed and upfront, explain every step, and tell you honestly when a visa isn't a good fit.

What clients can expect

  • A named adviser for your whole application
  • Secure document upload
  • Clear, fixed-fee quotes
  • Updates at every stage
